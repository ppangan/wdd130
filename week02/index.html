St. Barths

Saint Barthélemy, officially the Territorial Collectivity of Saint Barthélemy, is a picturesque overseas collectivity of France in the Caribbean. Often referred to as St. Barths, the island is renowned for its stunning white-sand beaches, designer boutiques, luxury yachts, and sophisticated blend of French culture and relaxed island living.
